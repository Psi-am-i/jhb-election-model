# Supporting this site

## Why the site exists

Johannesburg's council is elected under a mixed system: ward seats won outright,
proportional seats topped up from the party lists, and a statutory formula that
decides how the two combine. After the count comes the coalition arithmetic. Few
voters ever see any of it explained.

This site publishes a forecast of that council together with everything behind
it: the [method](methodology), the [sources](sources), the
[review](review) that found the model's errors, and the
[corrections](corrections). It is free to read, and free to redistribute with
attribution and a link back. The code and data are public on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Psi-am-i/jhb-election-model).

## Who may fund it

Support is accepted from two kinds of backer only:

- **institutional partners**, such as universities, research institutes, civic
  organisations and newsrooms;
- **named sponsors** that are not political parties.

The site does not take:

- donations;
- money or in-kind support from any political party, any candidate, or any of
  their office-bearers.

Every supporter is named on this page.

## What a supporter receives

An acknowledgement on this page: the supporter's name, and whether it is a
partner or a sponsor.

Support buys no editorial say. A supporter has no say over the model, its
inputs, its findings, the wording of any page, or when anything is published.

## Current supporters

None. When a supporter is accepted, its name is added here.
