# Supporting this site

## Why the site exists

Johannesburg's council is elected under a mixed system: ward seats won outright,
proportional seats topped up from the party lists, and a statutory formula that
decides how the two combine. After the count comes the coalition arithmetic.
This site exists to explain that system to voters and to forecast its result.

It publishes the forecast together with everything behind it: the
[method](methodology), the [sources](sources), the [review](review) that found
the model's errors, and the [corrections](corrections). It is free to read. Its
text, charts and published figures are licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): reuse them with
attribution and a link back. The model's code is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Psi-am-i/jhb-election-model) under the MIT licence.
The raw election results it starts from can be downloaded from the
[IEC](https://results.elections.org.za/).

## Who pays for it now

The site, its hosting and its advertising are paid for by its author through
picnic labs.

## Who may fund it

Support is accepted from two kinds of backer only:

- **institutional partners**, such as universities, research institutes, civic
  organisations and newsrooms;
- **named sponsors** that are not political parties.

The site does not take:

- donations;
- money or in-kind support from any political party, any candidate, any of
  their office-bearers, or any organisation they control, fund or that
  campaigns for or against a party or candidate.

Every supporter is named on this page, with the band its support falls in:
under R10,000, R10,000 to R50,000, or over R50,000.

## What a supporter receives

An acknowledgement on this page: the supporter's name, whether it is a partner
or a sponsor, and its amount band.

Support buys no editorial say. A supporter has no say over the model, its
inputs, its findings, the wording of any page, or when anything is published.

## Current supporters
