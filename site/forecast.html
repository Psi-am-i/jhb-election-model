Who can govern

If the DA rules out the ANC, the EFF and MK, its one realistic option is ActionSA and the PA: 16%▼ chance they have the numbers. The ANC without the DA has 10 workable majorities, 4 of them with a better than even chance of reaching 136 seats. This is arithmetic, not a judgement of which deal is likely.

About the model
Before the numbers

How to read this page

This forecast is 5,000 simulations of the election, each a plausible 2026 within the bounds of everything observed since 2006. A chance beside a party, a coalition or a result is the share of those 5,000 simulations in which it happens. "89%" never means a vote share or a poll figure: it means the thing happened in that share of the 5,000 simulations. The range beside each seat count is where the middle 90% of simulations landed.

Limits

What this forecast cannot tell you
  • Viability is not intent. The 43%▼ for ANC + DA is arithmetic, not a prediction that the deal is made.
  • A new party is sized from history, not from polling. Every party on the IEC's certified ballot is named in the simulations, and a party with no record is sized from how past newcomers performed. ActionSA went from absent to 44 seats in one cycle. This sizing can underestimate a breakthrough of that kind.
  • The ranges are the output. A seat number quoted without its range misreads the sheet. The shares of simulations are as good as the assumptions, which are set out on the methodology page.
  • The excessive-seats law is settled by precedent, not yet confirmed. A 2021 amendment completes Schedule 1, and the Electoral Commission's own Laingsburg 2021 calculation applies it. The Commission has been asked to confirm our reading.
  • This is not a poll. No voters were surveyed. It is arithmetic on results, registration and 15 by-elections. Polls are not an input; they are kept as an outside check.
