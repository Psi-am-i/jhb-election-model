Make your own forecast

Call every ward, set each party's share of the vote, and pick who governs. The seats follow from the law that seats the council. The page starts from the model's most likely winner in each ward.

Your forecast, and the name you type when you share it, are kept only in the page's address, so a link carries them. Nothing is sent or stored.

Set the vote

Each party's share of all votes, ward and list ballots together: the vote the law counts. The shares always add up to the whole. Moving one party scales the others in proportion. A party that wins a ward on your map keeps at least one vote.

The start shares give each party named in the published forecast its middle seat count, or its ward count where it is called for more wards than that. The middles need not add up to the council's size: the seats left over go to the other parties, in proportion to their average seats in the simulations.

Pick who governs

Choose the parties in the governing coalition. The parties that hold a seat in your council are listed.
