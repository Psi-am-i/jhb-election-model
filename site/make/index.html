Set the vote

Each party's share of all votes, ward and list ballots together: the vote the law counts. The shares always add up to the whole. Moving one party scales the others in proportion. A party that wins a ward on your map keeps at least one vote.

The start shares give each party its middle seat count from the published forecast, as nearly as the law allows. A party called for more wards than its middle starts at its ward count. The middles need not add up to the council's size, so some parties start one seat away from theirs.
