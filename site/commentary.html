Commentary

8 Aug
Nobody will win Johannesburg — and an obscure voting law will give the ANC more seats than expected
Johannesburg votes on two ballots at once, and they are added together. A party with tightly concentrated support can win more wards than its share entitles it to — and nobody unseats a ward winner. The shortfall is taken from every other party. The clause has fired exactly once before, in a Karoo town of seven seats.
8 Aug
When the ANC falls, nobody catches the votes
The opposition captured essentially none of the 587,000 votes the ANC bloc shed between the 2019 national election and the 2021 local election. The city's collapse in ANC support did not become anybody else's gain — it became abstention.
8 Aug
Minority administrations, and why nobody has to win
The mayor is elected by the councillors present and voting, so abstention installs governments. A council nobody controls is not a council nobody can run.
8 Aug
If the law were German instead
The same votes under our seat law and Germany's of 2000, 2015 and 2023.
