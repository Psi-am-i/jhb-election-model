Why the site exists

Johannesburg’s council is elected under a mixed system: ward seats won outright, proportional seats topped up from the party lists, and a statutory formula that combines the two. After the count comes the coalition arithmetic. This site explains that system and forecasts its result.

It publishes the forecast with the method, the sources, the review that found the model’s errors, and the corrections. It is free to read. Its text, charts and published figures are licensed CC BY 4.0: reuse them with attribution and a link back. The model’s code is on GitHub under the MIT licence. The raw election results are available from the IEC.

Who pays for it now

The site, its hosting and its advertising are paid for by its author through picnic labs.

Who may fund it

Support is accepted from two kinds of backer only:

  • institutional partners, such as universities, research institutes, civic organisations and newsrooms;
  • named sponsors that are not political parties.

The site does not take:

  • donations;
  • money or in-kind support from any political party, any candidate, any of their office-bearers, or any organisation they control or fund, or that campaigns for or against a party or candidate.

Every supporter is named on this page, with the band its support falls in: under R10,000, R10,000 to R50,000, or over R50,000.

What a supporter receives

An acknowledgement on this page: its name, whether it is a partner or a sponsor, and its amount band.

A supporter has no say over the model, its inputs, its findings, the wording of any page, or when anything is published.
