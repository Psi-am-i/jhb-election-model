MK will be the biggest party in the cityEXTREMELY UNLIKELY

Unlike most campaign talk this is a precise, checkable prediction: more seats than anyone else. MK is the largest party in 3.9%▲ of the 5,000 simulations.

  1. The gap is large, not marginal. MK's median is 17▼ seats of 270. Even at the top of its range — 55▲ seats, a result it beats in only one simulation in twenty — it is short of the ANC's typical 72▲ and the DA's 67▲.
  2. First place is a two-horse race in this forecast. The ANC alone has the most seats in 58%▲ of simulations and the DA alone in 34%▼. Two or more parties tie for the most seats in 3.6%▲. Everything else, MK included, shares what is left.
  3. The complaint about polls does not reach this forecast. Our model uses no poll at all. It is built from the record — how this city has actually voted, and the trajectories we can measure in that record. So the claim that pollsters undercount MK, whether or not it is right, leaves these numbers where they are. To be largest, MK would have to be under-represented in past ballot boxes, not in a survey — a different and much harder argument.
21 October 2026◆: what the model says now

Since then MK is the largest party in 3.9%▲ of simulations. Its median is 17▼ seats and the top of its range 55▲, against the ANC's 72▲ and the DA's 67▲.
