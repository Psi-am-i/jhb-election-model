A majority without the ANC, the EFF and MK is possible for the DAVERY LIKELY

A red line is a choice, so there is nothing here to call true or false. What can be tested is what it costs. Without the ANC, the EFF and MK, the rest of the council still clears 136 in 88% of simulations.

  1. There are three ways to a majority. The whole rest of the council except the ANC, which clears 136 in 88% of simulations; a deal with the ANC alone, 67%; or the narrower option, ActionSA and the PA, 8.6%.
  2. The first assumes the whole rest of the council acting as one, ActionSA, the PA and several small parties: the most generous and least stable option.
  3. The DA and the ANC together clear 136 in 67% of simulations. Neither party has said it wants it, but on this arithmetic it is the most reliable two-party majority in the city.
  4. The DA with ActionSA and the Patriotic Alliance reaches 136 in 8.6% of simulations. Across all simulations the three hold a median of 118 seats; where they reach a majority, a median of 140.
  5. The same test applied to the other side: an ANC-led council excluding the DA, the EFF and MK reaches 136 in 78% of simulations.
23 October 2026: what This Model says now

Since then the rest of the council, without the ANC, the EFF and MK, clears 136 in 88% of simulations. The DA and the ANC together clear it in 67%; the DA with ActionSA and the PA in 8.6%. The claim holds with a 88% chance, which the scale calls very likely. Correction, 23 October 2026: step 1 called the first route "the whole rest of the council except the ANC". Its figure is the rest of the council without the ANC, the EFF and MK together.
