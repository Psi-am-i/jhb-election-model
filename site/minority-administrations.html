The third way to govern

Minority administrations

No coalition table shows it, but the mayor is elected by councillors present and voting, so abstention installs minority governments — it is how the DA took this mayoralty in 2021. A minority administration is viable when its candidate outpolls the parties that actively vote against it, with everyone else abstaining. In the 5,000 simulations, that route is as open to the ANC as to the DA:

Candidate, and who votes against (everyone else abstains)Viable in, of simulations
DA minority, ANC+EFF+MK oppose4.0%
DA minority, only ANC opposes (2021 pattern)50%
ANC minority, only DA opposes48%
ANC minority, DA+ASA oppose13%
