The precedent — a dress rehearsal in the Karoo

Laingsburg, 2021: the whole mechanism in seven seats

Share this page

WhatsApp X

This clause is not a theory. The only case found in the Commission's seat calculations we searched is a Karoo town with a seven-seat council. In 2021 the DA won three of Laingsburg's four wards on just 24%▲ of the vote, a share entitling it to only two seats. The Electoral Commission's published calculation shows what the law does next: the DA kept all three wards and received no list seats; its votes were set aside and a fresh quota computed for everyone else over the four seats that remained; and the small Karoo Democratic Force lost the seat it would otherwise have held. The council stayed at seven. Scale that up thirty-eight-fold and you have this forecast's central case: the ANC winning roughly 74↘ of Johannesburg's wards on a 65↘-seat entitlement — keeping every one, taking zero list seats, and the list seats that remain re-divided among the other parties inside a council fixed at 270. A clause found in one small town's calculation is about to govern the country's biggest metro.

21 October 2026: what the model says now

The ANC now wins about 68 wards on average, against a median entitlement of 59 seats. The clause fires for the ANC: 90% chance.
