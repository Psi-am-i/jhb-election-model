# Pre-registration: the 2026 forecast reads the IEC's 26 September roll (2026-10-15)

Written before the re-emit and before any forecast or backtest run on the change.
Branch `roll-2026`. Owner approved the shortened process (one diff review, these
checks) on 2026-10-15.

## The change

Johannesburg 2026's registered voters per VD-ward part are rescaled so each
ward's total equals the IEC snapshot `archive/iec-registration-2026-09-26`
(ward rows), spread over the ward's parts in proportion to their 2024
registration. City total 2,348,781 → 2,417,915 (ward-row sum).

## Gates (any failure stops the round)

G1. Every backtest city-year (2011, 2016, 2021, eight metros) is unchanged:
    `compare_history` on the branch reproduces the tracked `history.json`
    bit for bit in every scored field, same draws and seed.
G2. The forecast run's summed registration equals 2,417,915, and each ward's
    equals its snapshot row.
G3. A test goes red when the forecast path is pointed back at the 2024 roll,
    and green on restore.

## Predictions (from the mechanism, not from any run)

Measured beforehand: summing each ward's roll change by the ward's current
favourite (`reports/model-changes/2026-10-14/f2026.json`, new model), wards the
DA is favourite in gain 51,023 registered voters (+5.8%), the EFF's +16,116
(+36%), MK's +4,245 (+11%), the PA's +4,368 (+6%), the ANC's −6,272 (−0.5%), the
IFP's −346 (−2%).

P1. List vote share: DA up, ANC down, by 0.2 to 1.5 points each. EFF up by under
    1 point.
P2. Mean seats: DA up 1 to 5, ANC down 1 to 5. No other party moves more than 3.
P3. Ward favourites change in under 10 wards, and the wards that change are
    among those whose roll changes most (|change| over 15%).
P4. The seat ranges (5th to 95th percentile) change width by under 3 seats for
    every party: the roll changes levels in wards, not the vote's variability.

Falsified if outside these ranges. Scored in the round's report, including what
it got wrong.
