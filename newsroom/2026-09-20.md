### 2. Loyiso Masuku tops ANC's Johannesburg PR list
- Business Day, 17 Sept 2026, "IEC final lists expose fierce metro battles ahead of local elections": reports Loyiso Masuku (ANC regional chairperson, executive deputy mayor) tops the party's certified Johannesburg PR list; Frank Chikane does not appear on the final list. — https://www.businessday.co.za/politics/elections-2026/2026-09-17-iec-final-lists-expose-fierce-metro-battles-ahead-of-local-elections/
- This is consistent with, and adds a harder data point to, PR #10's confirmation (8 Sept, Mbalula) that Chikane is off the list. Under SA's local-government convention, the PR list topper is typically also the party's mayoral candidate, making Masuku the ANC's presumptive Johannesburg mayoral candidate pending the party's own confirmation (still pending as of this run — no ANC national statement confirming her nomination was found).
