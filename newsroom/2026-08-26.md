## 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT: ANC defers naming its Joburg mayoral candidate, IEC deadline looms

**What happened.** The ANC's NEC met to approve final candidate lists but did
**not** announce its preferred metro mayoral candidates, including for
Johannesburg. Reporting says Ramaphosa argued that naming mayoral picks now —
especially in "highly contested municipalities like Johannesburg" — risked
losing contenders refusing to campaign for the eventual pick. The
still-shortlisted Johannesburg names are incumbent mayor Dada Morero, regional
chair Frank Chikane, and deputy mayor Loyiso Masuku; all will appear on the
ordinary councillor lists submitted to the IEC regardless of who is later
named the mayoral candidate.

- [Mayoral hopefuls await ANC list ranking decision — Sunday Times, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/politics/2026-08-25-mayoral-hopefuls-await-anc-list-ranking-decision/)
- [Why the ANC U-turned on announcing its mayoral candidates — Sunday Times, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/politics/2026-08-22-why-the-anc-u-turned-on-announcing-its-mayoral-candidates/)
- [ANC names 42 possible mayoral candidates for only eight metros — Daily Maverick, 22 Aug 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-08-22-anc-names-42-possible-mayoral-candidates-for-only-eight-metros/)

**Why it's model-relevant.** MODEL-LOG §1.1 already flags *contestation
coverage* (which parties field candidates in which wards) as a live 2026
input distinct from ticket-splitting. The IEC's candidate-nomination deadline
is **Friday 28 August 2026, 17:00** — no extensions — with final lists
published **16 September** and candidate certificates issued **25
September**. That 16 September publication is the point actual CoJ ward
contestation becomes verifiable data, not speculation, for every party
(ANC included, PA/ASA coverage gaps included). Worth a calendar note rather
than any model change today.
