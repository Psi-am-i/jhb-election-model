## 1. WATCH ITEM (not yet confirmed CoJ-relevant): ANC misses national candidate-nomination deadline

**What was found.** The IEC's Online Candidate Nomination System closed at
17:00 on 28 August 2026. Reporting from 1–3 September says 2,274 candidates
across 45 parties were captured but never formally submitted in time — 480
proportional-representation and 1,794 ward candidates. The IEC (chair
Mosotho Moepya) rejected the ANC's "technical glitch" explanation and
refused to reopen the system or grant an extension. The ANC also separately
dropped 350+ of its own councillor candidates after late vetting turned up
criminal records and missing matric certificates. An ANC member has asked
the Cape Town High Court to force the IEC to recognise him over a rival for
Ward 98, Khayelitsha — a Western Cape matter, not Johannesburg.

- [ANC candidate list issues hit three provinces before deadline — Business Day, 3 Sep 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/politics/2026-09-03-anc-candidate-list-issues-hit-three-provinces-before-deadline/)
- [IEC rejects ANC's 'technical glitch' excuse for missed candidate lists — News24, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.news24.com/politics/iec-rejects-ancs-technical-glitch-excuse-for-missed-candidate-lists-20260901-0794)
- [More than 2,200 candidates miss IEC deadline for local elections — Business Day, 2 Sep 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/news/2026-09-02-more-than-2200-candidates-miss-iec-deadline-for-local-elections/)
- [ANC drops hundreds of councillor candidates after last-minute vetting — African Insider](https://www.africaninsider.com/politics/anc-drops-hundreds-of-councillor-candidates-after-last-minute-vetting/)

**Why it matters if it does touch Joburg.** A ward where the ANC has no
valid candidate on the ballot is a ward the ANC forfeits outright —
categorically different from a poll-driven vote-share collapse and not
something the current model levers (δ/θ, `w_poll`, `w_bye`) capture. That
would be model-relevant in a way no polling shift is.

**Why it's flagged, not written up as a MODEL-LOG candidate.** Every
source this session could reach on the affected provinces names the
Eastern Cape, KwaZulu-Natal and the Free State. None of the readable
snippets name Gauteng or Johannesburg. One quote (an analyst, via Joburg
ETC/The Citizen) says ANC support is dropping "even in places like
Joburg" — that is commentary on the party's national mood, not evidence
of a missing Johannesburg candidate. Treat CoJ as unaffected until shown
otherwise.

## 2. One line worth knowing: CoJ ballot fragmentation, hard numbers

The IEC's 2 September release put the national candidate count at 142,072
for 10,526 council seats (a 43% increase on 2021), and named Johannesburg
as the country's single most contested municipality with a record 80
parties on its PR ballot. Not a lever change — the model already treats
ballot fragmentation as background — but a citable number if the digest
or site ever wants one. [80 political parties contest South Africa's
fiercest local elections battleground — SA People](https://www.sapeople.com/politics/80-political-parties-contest-south-africas-fiercest-local-elections-battleground-4-november-2026/)
