## 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT: final Joburg voter-roll count surfaces — closes MODEL-LOG §6's open registration item

**What was found.** Daily Maverick's "Five signs the ANC has given up on
Joburg" (1 Sep 2026) states the City of Johannesburg's voters' roll now
stands at **2.418 million registered voters — 35.8% of Gauteng's total**.
The same piece argues that even at a strong 58% turnout, most of Joburg's
non-voters would be disproportionately ANC-leaning, and repeats the
now-familiar asymmetry that DA-aligned voters turn out at local elections
at a higher rate than ANC-aligned voters.

- [Five signs the ANC has given up on Joburg — Daily Maverick, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-01-five-signs-the-anc-has-given-up-on-joburg/)

**Why it's model-relevant.** MODEL-LOG §6 lists as an open external item:
*"2026 registration-weekend figures, when published."* A final CoJ roll
total is exactly that figure. **2.418M** would be the first hard post-2024
registered-voter count for the city to check against whatever turnout-base
assumption `src/` currently uses (2021's roll was ~1.83M valid ward+PR votes
on a larger 2021 roll — the 2026 roll growing to 2.418M, if confirmed, is a
non-trivial base-size change worth a sanity check, not just a turnout-rate
one). The turnout-asymmetry point is not new information — POLLING.md
already treats SRF's turnout screen as favoring the DA — so that part is
corroboration, not a new lever.

**Caveat — verification gap.** This session's network egress to
`dailymaverick.co.za` (and to `news24.com`, `sabcnews.com`, `allafrica.com`,
and `iol.co.za`) was blocked, so the 2.418M figure and the 58%-turnout claim
are known only from search-engine result snippets of the article, not from
reading the full piece or its cited primary source (presumably the IEC's
own voters'-roll statistics). Before this figure goes anywhere near
`MODEL-LOG.md`, a human should confirm it directly against
elections.org.za's voter registration statistics page or the article
itself.
