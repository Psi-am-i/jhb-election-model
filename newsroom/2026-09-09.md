## 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT — Chikane confirmed off the list; not covered by the pending court challenge

**What was found.** Business Day and TimesLIVE (both 8 Sept 2026, "Chikane
left off ANC mayoral list in 'error', explains Mbalula") report that ANC
SG Fikile Mbalula, at a Luthuli House briefing, confirmed Reverend Frank
Chikane — along with Andile Lungisa (ANC's Nelson Mandela Bay mayoral
candidate) and Nocawe Mafu (Buffalo City) — was left off the relevant
candidate list due to an administrative lapse during the chaotic 28 August
submission window. This resolves, in the "left off" direction, the
contradiction PR #8 flagged as unconfirmed on 6 Sept.

Separately, TimesLIVE (8 Sept, "Court asked to declare ANC candidate lists
'validly submitted'") reports the ANC's Electoral Court application —
hearing set for 15 Sept 2026 — covers only six municipalities: Sundays
River Valley and Walter Sisulu (Eastern Cape party lists), Ngquza Hill and
Port St Johns (Eastern Cape party lists + ward nominations), uMshwathi
(KwaZulu-Natal party list), and Mangaung (Free State party list) — **no
Gauteng metro is named**. Total affected nationally: 181 candidates (130
PR, 51 ward) across those six councils.

**Why it matters.** Combining the two: Chikane's removal is confirmed, and
the one active legal avenue that might reinstate excluded ANC candidates
doesn't reach Johannesburg. Absent a separate, CoJ-specific challenge
(none reported), the ANC will contest Johannesburg's mayoralty without its
built-up presumptive challenger to Zille. This is categorically different
from a vote-share shift — it's a "candidate ineligible to sit" event the
model has no lever for, same gap PR #8 already named. It also reframes
the open question from "is Chikane on the list" (now answered: no) to "who
is the ANC's actual Johannesburg mayoral candidate" (unresolved — prior
reporting named Dada Morero and Loyiso Masuku alongside Chikane; no
confirmation which of them the party now leads with).
