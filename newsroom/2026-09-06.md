**What was found.** Coverage of the ANC's candidate-list chaos (Sunday
Times/TimesLIVE, "'Boss' Mbalula faces ANC backlash over election list

regional chair and one of three named mayoral hopefuls (with Dada Morero
and Loyiso Masuku) built up over months as the party's answer to Helen
Zille — in the context of the party's missed 28 August IEC nomination
deadline. Two irreconcilable readings surfaced in search snippets of the

  Luthuli House's registration chaos.
- Another reading: Chikane is "already safely registered on the IEC list
  as a councillor candidate" on the PR list, alongside Morero and Masuku.

**Why it matters.** This is precisely the "does the ANC's national list
mess reach a Johannesburg name" question PRs #6 and #7 flagged as
unconfirmed. If Chikane — the party's chosen mayoral challenger — is
genuinely off every list, he cannot sit as a councillor and therefore
cannot become mayor even if the ANC wins the most seats, regardless of any
δ/θ or polling-lever reading. If he's registered, this is a non-event and
the earlier "watch item" narrows to municipalities outside Gauteng. The
model currently has no lever for "chosen mayoral candidate ineligible to
sit," which is categorically different from a vote-share shift.

## 2. Context, not itself CoJ-specific: Electoral Court closes the door on deadline exceptions

The Electoral Court gave written reasons (4 Sept 2026) for its 28 August
order dismissing the newly-registered United Democratic Front party's bid
to be excused from the ward-nomination deadline, holding that IEC Act
s11(2)(a) permits only a general timetable amendment "necessary for a free
and fair election" and confers no power to exempt one party from a deadline
binding all others. UDF is not confirmed to contest any Johannesburg ward.
Relevance: this is a precedent against any ANC bid (Ramaphosa has floated
approaching the Electoral Court, per PR #7) to get late candidates —
Chikane included, if the "left off" reading is correct — reinstated by
court order. Source (snippet only, egress blocked):
[TimesLIVE, "IEC had no power to condone UDF Party's late ward nominations, court rules," 4 Sep 2026](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-04-iec-had-no-power-to-condone-udf-partys-late-ward-nominations-court-rules/)
