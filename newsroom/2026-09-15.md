## Items

### 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT — Chikane/ANC deny litigation over Joburg mayoral list
Frank Chikane and ANC national spokesperson Mahlengi Bhengu both denied
reports that Chikane is taking the ANC to court over his omission from the
party's Johannesburg mayoral candidate lists, calling the report "fake" and
an attempt to sow division ahead of the election. This is distinct from
Andile Lungisa's own, separate court challenge, which concerns his removal
from the ANC's **Nelson Mandela Bay** list, not Johannesburg.
Sources: [News24, 14 Sept](https://www.news24.com/politics/frank-chikane-anc-dismiss-claims-that-he-is-taking-the-party-to-court-20260914-0621),
[IOL, 14 Sept](https://iol.co.za/news/politics/2026-09-14-frank-chikane-denies-anc-court-action-over-johannesburg-mayoral-bid/),
[EWN, 14 Sept](https://www.ewn.co.za/2026/09/14/frank-chikane-denies-that-he-s-taking-anc-to-court-over-candidate-list-exclusion).

### 2. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT — Johannesburg has the most PR-ballot contestants of any metro
Reporting on the IEC's finalised candidate lists states Johannesburg has 80
parties contesting the proportional-representation ballot — more than
Tshwane (72), eThekwini (70), or Ekurhuleni (69). This is new corroborating
evidence for MODEL-LOG's R7 register item (new-entrant/vote-splintering
risk, currently "🟡 structural, quantified in fold 1") — it doesn't change
any model output on its own, but it's a concrete, sourced number a reviewer

Source: [Daily Maverick, 13 Sept](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-13-voters-splinter-among-parties-as-support-for-the-anc-plunges/)
(reporting IEC figures).
