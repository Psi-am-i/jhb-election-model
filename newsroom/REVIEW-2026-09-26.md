# What is in the fifteen digests, and what can be used

Read 2026-09-26, after the digests were brought into the working line. Every
figure below is **reported**, not measured: it is what a news outlet said, and
the digest that carried it usually could not reach the primary source. Where
this document states something measured on Atlas it says so.

---

## 1. The poll that is not in the register

**Ipsos Khayabus, CoJ.** Reported 4 September 2026, from a national omnibus
(n≈3,600 across nine provinces) with a metro breakout. The CoJ subsample size is
not stated anywhere the digests could reach.

| party | reported |
|---|---|
| ANC | 31% |
| DA | 27% or 28% — **disputed between outlets** |
| ActionSA | 14% |
| EFF | 8% |
| MK | 6% |

The ANC/DA line comes from Daily Maverick and allAfrica (digest 2026-09-05);
Bloomberg reports DA 28 from the same release (2026-09-08); the ActionSA/EFF/MK
split comes from Fullview, citing the same release (2026-09-13). Nobody in the
chain reached Ipsos's own release.

**Why it matters.** This is the first non-SRF, non-party-internal CoJ read since
March, and it swings sharply toward the ANC against every other 2026 CoJ number
on file. It is not in `polls.json`.

⚠️ **Polls left the forecast on 12 September 2026** (MODEL-LOG §1.244), so
adding it does not move the published forecast. It bears on `POLLING.md`'s
honest-lever spread and on the trust ceiling still open as HANDOVER B1.

⛔ **Do not adopt the DA figure until the 27/28 dispute is settled against the
primary release.** One point is inside anyone's margin and outside the
register's tolerance for a number with two published values.

## 2. Four claims that name a quantity this model already computes

Each is a claim by a named person, on the record, that maps to a probability the
forecast can state. None has been tested; none needs new machinery.

| # | speaker, date | claim | the quantity that tests it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dickson Masemola (ANC NEC), 14 Aug | the ANC can govern Johannesburg alone, given "absolute authority" | P(ANC holds a majority alone) |
| 2 | Bongani Baloyi (MK mayoral candidate), 9 Sept | "we are going to be the biggest party in the city"; polls underpoll MK | P(MK is the largest single party) |
| 3 | Helen Zille (DA mayoral candidate), 11 Sept | rules out coalition with the **EFF** and MK | P(a sufficient coalition exists that excludes the EFF and MK) |
| 4 | Helen Zille, 11 Sept | "the biggest obstacle to us getting a majority in Joburg is DA supporters who don't go and vote" | P(DA majority \| DA-leaning turnout raised to its historic ceiling) |

Claim 4 is the one worth building first: it is a turnout lever, and the
interactive is being built around exactly that control. It converts a party
leader's explanation of her own defeat into a number a reader can move.

⚠️ Claim 3 is a paraphrase. Daily Maverick was unreachable from every session
that tried, so the quote has never been read in its source. Claim 1's wording
likewise comes from secondary reporting.

## 3. Two checks on the model's own inputs

**The roster: 82 parties.** The digests report the IEC's certified CoJ PR ballot
at **82 parties** (2026-09-17), then a dispute — 82 against 75 — the following
week (2026-09-20). Measured on Atlas: `pools_2026.json` carries a roster of
**82** parties with `roster_source = "declared"`, and the declared roster is
marked COMPLETE, which is what licenses dropping the 27 parties holding 1.70% of
the 2021 vote. The model and the IEC report agree at 82. **The 82/75 dispute has
not been resolved against elections.org.za, and the model's licence to delete
parties rests on the number being right.**

**The roll: the model is using 2021's.** The digests report a final CoJ
registered figure of **2.418M** (2026-09-02, unverified against the IEC).
Measured on Atlas: the model's turnout denominator is read from the 2021 results
file — **2,220,710 registered across 867 voting districts**. There is no 2026
registration file in `data/raw/` at all. The reported 2026 roll is **8.9%
larger** than the roll the model divides by, across 872 VDs rather than 867.

⛔ This is a check to run, not a defect established here. Whether the gap reaches
the forecast depends on whether turnout enters as a ratio carried forward or as a
count against a denominator, and this review did not establish which.

  Johannesburg — relevant to the portal's second city, not to this forecast.

  city-wide (2026-09-04), against the 82 above. The two counts are from
  different dates and different stages of certification.

## 5. What the digests could not do, three runs in four

`dailymaverick.co.za`, `bloomberg.com`, `timeslive.co.za`, `iol.co.za`,
`news24.com` and `ipsos.com` were all unreachable from the routine's sessions.
Every poll figure above therefore rests on a search snippet rather than an
article, and the digests say so each time. The egress allowlist is the single
change that would most improve what the routine can establish.
