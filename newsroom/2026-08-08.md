## 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT: election date proclaimed, voters' roll closed

COGTA Minister Velenkosini Hlabisa gazetted the proclamation of the local
government election date on **7 August 2026** — Government Gazette No.
55162, issued under s24(1) of the Local Government: Municipal Structures Act
— formally setting **4 November 2026**. This starts the IEC's 89-day
election timetable. The national voters' roll closed at midnight on 7
August; candidate nominations are open until **28 August 2026, 17:00**.

This is the step up from "expected"/"announced" (Ramaphosa, 30 April) to a
gazetted, statutory instrument. It's the trigger for MODEL-LOG §6's open
item ("External — 2026 registration-weekend figures, when published") —
national totals are now out (see item 2), though a CoJ-specific figure
hasn't been published yet.

## 2. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT: national/provincial voter-roll growth published (partial)

IEC figures from the final registration weekend (1–2 August): 1,794,051
transactions nationally (291,806 first-time voters), roll now over 29
million, +2.1 million since the 2024 elections. Gauteng recorded 314,856
transactions in that weekend — second-highest of the nine provinces — plus
86,000 youth registrations. **No City of Johannesburg-specific breakdown
was found in this scan** — worth a follow-up search once the IEC or a metro
outlet publishes the CoJ-level roll size, since that's the turnout
denominator the model needs.
