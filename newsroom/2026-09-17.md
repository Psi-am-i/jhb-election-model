## 1. MODEL-RELEVANT EVENT: Final, certified candidate lists confirm Johannesburg at 82 PR-ballot parties — double-column ballot triggered

**What happened.** The IEC published finalised candidate lists on 16
September 2026. Johannesburg draws the largest PR-ballot field of any
metro — **82 parties**, ahead of Tshwane (72), eThekwini (70), and
Ekurhuleni (69) — up from the 80 reported pre-certification in PR #13
(15 Sept). Because more than 48 parties are contesting, Johannesburg's PR
ballot requires a **double-column ballot paper**. Nationally the IEC
certified 136,790 candidates, versus 95,427 in 2021.

## 2. One-liner: Electoral Court ANC candidate-exclusion ruling — confirmed not a CoJ matter

The Electoral Court on 16 September dismissed the ANC's appeal to reinstate
181 candidates excluded over a missed 28 August submission deadline. The six
affected municipalities are named explicitly: Port St Johns, Ngquza Hill,
Walter Sisulu, Sundays River Valley (Eastern Cape), uMshwathi (KZN), and
Mangaung (Free State) — **City of Johannesburg is not among them**,
confirming PR #12's (14 Sept) provisional read of the same underlying
dispute. Thread closed; no CoJ action needed.
