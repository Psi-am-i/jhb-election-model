# Newsdesk digest — 2026-09-13

## Summary
- MK Party's Joburg mayoral candidate Bongani Baloyi publicly predicts MK will be "the biggest party" in the city, dismissing polls that put MK at ~6% CoJ — a new testable claim, not previously logged.
- IEC's CoJ multiple-list resolution deadline passed 11 Sept 5pm; certified final candidate lists for all municipalities (Joburg included) are now due **16 Sept** — the date that will settle whether Frank Chikane is actually on/off the ANC's Joburg list (open since PR #8).
- A new Gauteng High Court challenge by ANC NEC member Andile Lungisa against SG Mbalula is about **Nelson Mandela Bay, not Johannesburg** — resolves an ambiguity carried since PR #9 (Lungisa was previously grouped with Chikane) — but Lungisa's court papers allege Mbalula personally ordered list deletions at a Johannesburg hotel meeting, corroborating detail for the still-unresolved Chikane story.
- A fuller breakdown of the Ipsos poll already logged in PR #9 has surfaced: CoJ ANC 31 / DA 28 / ActionSA 14 / EFF 8 / MK 6 — same release, additional party figures only, not a new wave.
- Herman Mashaba skipped Radio 702's 10 Sept Joburg mayoral debate; Zille used the absence to attack his mayoral record — campaign colour, no new numeric claim.
