What changed, and what it did

No turnout tilt from by-elections

In the current forecast, by-election results raise or lower expected turnout in 79 of 825 voting districts, by between 9% down and 14% up. The new model sets every one to no change.

Nothing measurable moves

No party’s list vote moves (largest change under 0.001 points), no ward changes favourite, and no median seat moves. The ANC is the largest party alone in 58.4% of simulations (was 58.1%), the DA in 33.5% (was 33.8%).

Past elections

No backtest: neither input is tested by any backtest.
