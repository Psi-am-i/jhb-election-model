What changed, and what it did

PA, Al Jama-ah and AIC get their real ward patterns

γ says how much of a party’s pattern across voting districts carries over from one election to the next: 1 is all of it, 0 is none. The γ file for 2014 → 2016 lists every party, including those it could not fit, written as 1. The model read those rows as fits, so PA was drawn at 1.0 instead of its fit from 2021 → 2024 (0.544), and Al Jama-ah at 1.0 instead of 0.887. Two parties with no votes in 2016 (Minority Front, NFP) were drawn at 0 and now take 1. Nothing else changes.

Four wards move from PA to the DA

Wards 17, 18, 68 and 82 change favourite from PA to the DA, and ward 124 from MK to the ANC. PA’s votes are spread more evenly across the city, so it leads in fewer wards.

The ANC gains 3 points in the race for largest party

The ANC is the largest party alone in 61.2% of simulations (was 58.1%), the DA in 30.9% (was 33.8%). No party’s median seats move by more than 1, and every party’s list vote is unchanged.
