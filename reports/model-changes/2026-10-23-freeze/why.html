What changed, and what it did

ActionSA: 12.7% of the list vote, from 17.6%

The 17.6% on the site since 21 October was your figure, not one the model estimated. The model’s own figure was 12.6% throughout, and withdrawing your call puts it back: a change of input, not new evidence about ActionSA. ActionSA’s 90% range is 8.4% to 17.9%. Its level is a fall from its 2021 result of 18.1%, and most of that fall comes from one measured number applied to a party it was not measured on: the model assumes ActionSA keeps 72% of its first result, read from a line fitted to 292 pairs of consecutive local results across all parties, mostly established ones, because ActionSA has only one local election of its own. Parties at their second local election have kept from 0.2 to 1.5 of their first result, and that record could not say which ActionSA will be (OWNER-DECISIONS item 34). Median seats 29, from 40.

Its 5 points go to every party in proportion, and the DA gets the seats

Every other party’s list vote rises by the same 6%: DA 26.7% (was 25.2%), ANC 22.5% (21.2%), EFF 10.1% (9.6%), MK 9.1% (8.5%), PA 6.6% (6.2%). The DA’s median seats go to 71 (was 67). The ANC’s stay at 72: it already wins more wards than its list vote entitles it to in 87% of simulations (was 90%), and in those its seats are its ward wins, so extra list vote adds none. A party winning a majority alone: 2 simulations in 5,000.

Which change did what

Pooling γ and fixing the defect, without the ActionSA change, moved the ANC’s chance of being largest alone from 58.1% to 59.1% and the DA’s from 33.8% to 32.9% (the pre-freeze test of 23 October). Withdrawing the ActionSA call did the rest: ANC 59.1% → 45.1%, DA 32.9% → 46.7%.

Seven wards change favourite

Wards 9 and 10 go to Al Jama-ah (from the DA and the ANC). Al Jama-ah won ward 9 in 2021 with 49% and took 21% of ward 10; the site’s forecast missed this because a placeholder in the γ file flattened Al Jama-ah’s pattern. Ward 10 at 51% may overstate it. Wards 17, 18, 68 and 82 move from PA to the DA, for the same reason in reverse: PA’s real pattern is more even than the placeholder said. Ward 124, a close MK–ANC ward, moves to the ANC. Wards were redrawn for 2026, so the 2021 figures are a guide, not a like-for-like comparison.
