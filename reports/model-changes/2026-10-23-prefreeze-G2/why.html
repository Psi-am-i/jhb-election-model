What changed, and what it did

γ from the most recent pair of elections

The largest changes: EFF ward 1.29 (was 0.95), ACDP 0.70 (was 0.39), PAC 0.77 (was 0.28), Al Jama-ah 1.53 (was 1.0). The ANC and DA move by under 0.04. Two fitted values are negative, AIC −0.09 and COPE −0.02 on the ward ballot, which would mean their support swaps places across the city; they are used as fitted. APC and AZAPO have no 2019 → 2021 fit and take 1.0.

Ward favourites

Wards 9 and 10 move to Al Jama-ah (from the DA and the ANC). Wards 17, 18, 68 and 82 move from PA to the DA, which is the defect fix, not γ.

The race for largest party

The ANC is the largest party alone in 56.7% of simulations (58.1% now, 61.2% with only the defect fixed), the DA in 34.9% (33.8%, 30.9%). No median seat moves by more than 1, and no party’s list vote moves: γ changes where a party’s votes fall, not how many it has.
