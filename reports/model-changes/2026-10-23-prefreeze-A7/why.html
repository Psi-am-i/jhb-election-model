What changed, and what it did

By-elections no longer move party levels

In the current forecast, each party’s starting level is pulled halfway toward what the by-elections since 2021 imply. The new model drops that pull and the turnout effect. ActionSA does not move: it is held at the 17.6% you set on 21 October, which the by-elections do not reach.

The ANC gains, the DA and PA lose

List vote: ANC 22.15% (was 21.24%), DA 24.30% (was 25.16%), EFF 10.27% (was 9.56%), PA 5.56% (was 6.20%), IFP 1.40% (was 1.61%).

The race for largest party moves 14 points

The ANC is the largest party alone in 72.1% of simulations (was 58.1%), the DA in 20.6% (was 33.8%). Median seats: ANC 74 (was 72), DA 64.5 (was 67), EFF 27 (was 25). Ward 124 changes favourite from MK to the ANC.

Coalitions

ANC + ActionSA + EFF has a majority in 67.3% of simulations (was 56.3%), ANC + ActionSA + MK in 42.5% (was 36.9%). ANC + DA is unchanged at 56%.

Past elections

No backtest: neither input is tested by any backtest. The by-election weight of one half was chosen on the 2016–2021 by-elections against the 2021 result; that test saw the result it was scored on.
