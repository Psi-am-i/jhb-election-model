Privacy notice: predictions at joburg.whysoserious.city

This notice explains what we keep when you sign in to save and share a prediction of the Johannesburg council election of 4 November 2026, why, for how long, who processes it for us, and how to delete it.

Information Officer

[Information Officer: name, email address, registration with the Information Regulator]. You may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa), [Regulator's current contact details].

What we keep

  • Your sign-in: which service you used (Google, Apple, Microsoft, Facebook or X) and the account id that service gives us for you. We do not collect your name, photo, friends or location from any of them.
  • One email address, only once it is confirmed. If your sign-in service tells us it has verified your address (Google, Apple and X can), we keep it. Otherwise, if you give us an address, we send it one email with a link to confirm it and keep the address only if the link is used. We never email an unconfirmed address apart from that one link.
  • Your predictions: the ward winners, vote shares and coalition you chose, the seats our seat calculation gives them, and the dates they were made and changed.
  • A label for each shared prediction. We do not collect your name. A prediction shows your X @username if you signed in with X and keep it, a random label such as "pollster-23" (chosen afresh for each prediction), or words you type yourself. Your X username is stored only if you keep it as a label.
  • Your choices on the consent screen, each with the date and a code identifying the exact wording you were shown.
  • We do not store your IP address. Our service provider sees it when you connect (below).

Why, and on what basis

  • To save your prediction, show it at its link, score it against the result, and analyse saved predictions against the result and against past elections. Basis: your consent (POPIA section 11(1)(a)), given by the box "Save my prediction".
  • To send the one email after the election described below, and anything else you ticked (telling you if your prediction wins; updates). Basis: your consent; updates are electronic direct marketing and are sent only if you opt in (section 69).
  • To keep the service working and safe: bot checks, rate limits, error logs. Basis: our legitimate interest (section 11(1)(f)).

Our view of what a prediction is. A saved prediction is a forecast of how the election will turn out, not a statement of how you vote or what you believe. The responsible party therefore treats it as ordinary personal information, not special personal information under section 26, and the protections above (explicit consent, separate unticked options, 18 or older, deletion at any time) apply anyway. You must be 18 or older to save a prediction.

Who can see it

Anyone with the link to a prediction can see it, with its label. Links are not listed anywhere by us and are marked so that search engines do not index them. If you share a link, the people and services you share it with can see it and may keep a copy, including the preview image.

How long we keep it

  • After the election, on [Post-election message date], we send one email to your confirmed address, whatever you ticked, asking whether to delete your data now or keep your address to tell you about the next election.
  • If you do not answer, your account, predictions and address are deleted on [Deletion date]. If we hold no confirmed address for you, we cannot send that email, and your account and predictions are deleted on that date without it.
  • If you ask to hear about the next election, we delete your predictions on that date and keep only your sign-in id and address until you ask us to stop.
  • You can delete any prediction, or your whole account, at any time from "My forecasts". You do not need an email address to delete. A deleted prediction's link stops working at once.
