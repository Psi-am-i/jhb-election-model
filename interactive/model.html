Polls

Our setting is zero trust: the published forecast uses no polls. Moving the slider is your judgement, not ours.

These are the polls of Johannesburg the model's own rules admit. At full trust each poll counts with its own sampling error; at half trust with twice that error; at zero it has no weight at all. Full trust never means the poll comes true: the figures then describe the model's draws nearest the polls.

Weight of recent by-elections

How much the model leans on the ward by-elections held since the last local election. Each setting is its own full run of the model. The published weight is an unmeasured judgement, recorded in the model's register of judgement calls, that only the November result can test.

Computed in your browser from your settings.

By-elections are small, low-turnout contests, and some swings rest on a handful of wards. The swing column is measured in the wards that held by-elections only.

The model's column is its central level for each party at this weight: the deterministic value its simulations are drawn around, before any simulation is drawn. Levels are not vote shares and do not add up to a whole, so a level is compared only with the same party's level at the published weight, never with a drawn share or with the swing column.

The three settings are separate runs, not the same simulations re-weighted, so part of any difference between them is sampling noise. A change in a party's middle seat count is marked "within sampling noise" when it is smaller than twice the combined standard error of the two settings' middle counts.
