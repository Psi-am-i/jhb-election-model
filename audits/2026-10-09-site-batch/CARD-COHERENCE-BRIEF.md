# Brief: one chance on the reader's card (owner, 2026-10-12)

QC.md §4b. Approved by the owner 2026-10-12 ("go ahead with card change").

## The defect
A reader's share card printed "We give this outcome a 45% chance" (the
council-level outcome, `reweight.council_outcome`: largest party, majority,
coalition) beside a "Wild call <15%" badge, which comes from the forecast's
boldest single call (`reweight.boldest_call`, here Truth and Solidarity
Movement at 1 seat). Two different chances, printed as if one. Owner: "we need
to have this internally coherent."

## Items
1. **One chance on the card.** The card's chance line states the boldest
   call's own chance, the same number the badge bands:
   "Your boldest call: <party> at <n> seats. We give that <x> chance."
   (`share-card.js` `boldestLine`/`chanceSentence`; the arrival view's "lead"
   wording kept). The council-outcome chance leaves the card. It stays in
   "Analyse my forecast", labelled as the chance of that overall result
   (largest party / majority / coalition), not "this outcome". A forecast
   equal to the published prediction keeps a line saying so and the Safe bet
   badge.
2. **One-seat calls do not set the badge.** A party whose seats in the
   reader's forecast are 1 or fewer AND whose seats in the published
   prediction are 1 or fewer is skipped when choosing the boldest call. One
   rule for every party (no party named in code). Declare it in
   JUDGEMENT-CALLS.md as an amendment to §N10, concretely.
3. **Measured from the published prediction.** The "middle" a reader's call is
   measured from is the published prediction the site shows (find its single
   definition and artefact; do not create a second one), not a per-party
   median computed separately, unless they are provably the same object, in
   which case say so and change nothing here.

## Constraints
- No forecast number changes. Model code (`src/montecarlo.py`, `src/pools.py`)
  untouched.
- One definition: `src/reweight.py` and `worker-api/seats-py/src/reweight.py`
  must stay identical by the repo's existing sync mechanism (find it; do not
  hand-edit two copies apart). Same for interactive copies.
- Existing sealed links keep working; a card regenerated from an old link
  shows the new wording.
