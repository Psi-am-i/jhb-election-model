# 1b v3 addendum 2: the σ_cyc estimator was in the wrong frame

Raised by the level-lift design (`1e-level-lift-design.md` §3.3). Checked here
with numbers. **The point is right and it is a defect.** The corrected estimator
and widths are below, at the owner's 1% line (`UNIT_LINE_SHARE` = 0.01, addendum
1). Read-only; no writing runs; no sub-delegation.

## 1. The defect

v3's draw is `logistic(logit m + κ + x)`, x ~ N(0, s²), with κ chosen so that
E[share] = m. For a small cell, log(share/m) ≈ κ + x with κ ≈ −s²/2. The
reviewer measured κ = −0.44 at s = 0.93, against −s²/2 = −0.43. So **if the
centres are right in the mean, the log residuals the record shows have mean
−s²/2, not 0.**

v3 §3.3 set σ_cyc as the RMS of the per-cycle mean log residuals about **0**.
That reads the draw's own −s²/2 offset as cycle variation, and it counts
differently by cycle: a cycle with mean −0.5 is not evidence of any cycle
effect, yet it adds 0.5² to σ_cyc².

**Simulation check.** True σ_cyc 0.50, σ_btw 0.18, σ_wit 0.73 (total 0.90);
three cycles; 8 metros and about 60 parties per cycle; 20,000 repetitions;
residuals generated by the draw itself:

| estimator | median | root mean square |
|---|---|---|
| v3: RMS of cycle means about 0 | 0.595 | 0.654 |
| corrected: about −s²/2 (below) | 0.442 | 0.500 |

The v3 estimator is biased upward by about 0.15 at these values. The corrected
one is unbiased in mean square, which is the E[k²] quantity CLAUDE.md §4 asks
for.

## 2. The corrected estimator

Residuals r = log(realised / `pool_spec` centre). Per cycle t, let `m_t` be the
mean residual. `σ_btw` and `σ_wit` are unchanged: they are deviations from cycle
and metro means, so the offset cancels. The cycle component is measured about
the draw's own expected log residual:

    σ_cyc² = mean over earlier cycles of (m_t + s²/2)²,   s² = σ_cyc² + σ_btw² + σ_wit²

Substituting gives a quadratic in q = s²:

    q²/4 + (m̄ − 1)·q + (mean m_t² + σ_btw² + σ_wit²) = 0

where m̄ is the mean of the `m_t`. Take the smaller root,
q = 2[(1 − m̄) − √((1 − m̄)² − mean m_t² − σ_btw² − σ_wit²)], and
σ_cyc = √(q − σ_btw² − σ_wit²).

**When the quadratic has no root**, no mean-preserving lognormal reproduces both
the residuals' mean and their spread. In that case the centres are biased in the
mean by more than any width can explain. The writer then sets the width to the
sd about the observed mean, and records the mean bias as a level defect report;
it is never folded into the width. In the simulation this happens in 3.9% of
repetitions.

The debutant width is the same estimator with no cycle split:
q²/4 + (μ − 1)q + mean r² = 0, where μ is the mean residual.

## 3. Corrected widths at 1%

Inputs: per-cycle established components at 1% (addendum 1 §5). Cycle means
−0.971 / −0.191 / +0.495; within 0.64 / 0.63 / 0.84; between 0.00 / 0.27 / 0.14;
n 36 / 58 / 75.

| width for | v3 at 1% (about 0): σ_cyc / total | **corrected: σ_cyc / σ_btw / σ_wit / total** | offset −s²/2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 (from 2011) | 0.971 / 1.163 | **0.591 / 0.00 / 0.64 / 0.871** | −0.38 |
| 2021 (from 2011-2016) | 0.700 / 0.963 | **0.465 / 0.19 / 0.63 / 0.809** | −0.33 |
| 2026 (from 2011-2021) | 0.639 / 0.988 | **0.661 / 0.18 / 0.73 / 1.002** | −0.50 |

The direction differs by year:
- **2016 and 2021 fall.** Their earlier cycles' means (−0.97, −0.19) sit near or
  below −s²/2, which is what a correct centre produces, so less of them is cycle
  variation.
- **2026 rises slightly.** 2021's +0.495 sits 0.99 above −s²/2 = −0.50.

Debutants (residuals at 1%: n 16 / 28 pooled / 68 pooled; mean +0.31 / −0.10 /
−0.08):

| width for | v3 at 1% (RMS about 0) | **corrected** |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 0.94 | **0.914**: no root, so sd about the observed mean; mean bias +0.31 recorded as a level report |
| 2021 | 1.29 | **1.310**: no root, so sd about the observed mean; mean bias −0.10 |
| 2026 | 0.95 | **1.066** |

2011 keeps v3's fallback (0.27 / 0.48), untestable.

## 4. What it changes in the predictions

Panel coverage at 1% (the falsifier population; out of sample, about the
`pool_spec` centre):

| | n | addendum 1 | **corrected** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 71 | 0.915 | **0.901** (5 below, 2 above) |
| 2021 | 117 | 0.855 | **0.829** (2 below, 18 above) |
| 2016 + 2021 | 188 | 0.878 | **0.856** |

Falsifier 4 becomes: outside **0.901 ± 0.07** (2016) or **0.829 ± 0.07** (2021).
The rest of addendum 1's table moves little. The 2026 established width goes
0.988 → 1.002 and the debutant width 0.95 → 1.07, so the JHB 2026 tail counts
rise slightly from addendum 1's figures (11 draws above 5%; rate above 2%
0.0067). The writer's first run should restate them. Falsifier 3 (more than 40
draws) is not at risk.

## 5. Artefact key (level-lift designer's requirement, adopted)

The key of `data/processed/unit_width_record.json` must include:
- `level_shrink` and `level_shrink_scale`, the scenario values the centres were
  built with;
- the source hash of `compress_levels`;
- the existing hash of the centres' other inputs.

Supporting changes:
- the writer takes a scenario override and an `--out` path, so a record with
  `level_shrink = 0.0` can sit beside the default;
- `make_drawer` refuses a record whose key does not match the run's scenario.

This replaces v3 §3.5's "artefact key over the centres' inputs" with an explicit
list.
