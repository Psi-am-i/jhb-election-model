## B. Methodology corrections (docs-public/methodology.md, JUDGEMENT-CALLS.md Plain fields)

Text between `__JUDGEMENTS_START__`/`__JUDGEMENTS_END__` is generated by
src/judgement_sheet.py from JUDGEMENT-CALLS.md "Plain" fields: edit the Plain
field, then regenerate (`judgement_sheet.py`, `model_index.py`,
`site_text.py --export`). Do not change any model number or code path.

1. §H2 Plain (turnout), replace with:
   "*Turnout in each group starts at its 2021 level.* Each population group's
   expected turnout is its turnout at the 2021 local election, with a range
   measured from how group turnout has moved between local elections in the
   eight metros. How a group's voters spread across voting districts comes from
   each district's fall from the national to the local election in 2016 and
   2021, with 2021 counting for 70%; that weight is from the original plan and
   has not been tested. On your forecast you can move any or all four groups.
   A group you leave alone stays at the forecast's turnout."
   Check each clause against src/turnout.py and src/pools.py (turnout_band,
   panel_turnout_spread) before writing it; report any clause that is not true.
2. methodology.md ~l.121 "the lowest on record": false (each pool's
   observed_low in pools_2026.json is below its 2021 figure). Write "Turnout
   fell in every group in 2021, so the range allows falls as well as rises."
3. Step 4, "drawn from what turnout has done in that city before": the width is
   measured across the eight metros (pools.panel_turnout_spread); only the
   centre is the city's. Say that.
4. By-elections Plain (JUDGEMENT-CALLS ~l.1133, "Nobody knows the right
   weight, so the two count equally"): MODEL-LOG §1.325/§1.326 tested it on
   2021; equal weight gave the smallest list-vote error, with 0.4–0.6 within
   0.3 points. Say that plainly; the 0.3 points is a measurement, so it is a
   token or it is left out.
5. Other-cities/validation text, "nothing is carried across from
   Johannesburg": false. The turnout range and the turnout correlation are
   measured across the metros, Johannesburg included. Say that.
6. The generated validation block (src/build_validation.py): reader text must
   not name src/… or data/… files or "a 400-draw run". Say "Tested on
   30 September 2026 against an earlier version of This Model, on 400
   simulations" (date and count from the artefact, not typed). Re-running it
   is NOT part of this change.
7. "337 times … 2006 to 2021 … eight metros" (l.278): make 337 a fixed token
   in content/joburg/stats.toml with its source (MODEL-LOG §1.296), per the
   stat-provenance rule.
8. "before the draws" → "before the simulations". Where the page says "the
   model" meaning this model, write "This Model". Leave a defined term
