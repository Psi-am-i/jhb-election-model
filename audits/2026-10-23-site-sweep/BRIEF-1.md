## D. The correction entries

Format, as agreed and as `docs-public/corrections.md` has it now:

    ## <span data-asof="DATE">"the wrong words"</span> — published D1, corrected D2

    **What was wrong.** One or two sentences: which page said what.

    **What is right.** What it says now.

Nothing else in the body. D1 is the first day the wrong text was on the live
site: check it against the live editions (`archive/live-deployed-*`, the
ledger's deploy rows, `wrangler deployments list`), never a commit or a build
that was not deployed. D2 is the go-live date of this round. Newest first.
Entries for C1-C5, C7, C9-C12, C15, and for every B item whose wrong text was
live (B1, B9, B10, B11, B12, B17, B25 at least; check each).
